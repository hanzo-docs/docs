# Hanzo OS Mastery: Agentic Curriculum

Welcome to Hanzo University. This curriculum guides you from foundational agentic concepts to advanced deployment of autonomous stacks.

## Curriculum Path

1. **Introduction to Autonomous Stacks**
   - Understanding the Hanzo Stack architecture.
   - Core concepts of agentic CRM and autonomous operations.
2. **Hanzo OS Fundamentals**
   - `hanzo up` environment setup and management.
   - Core CLI tools.
3. **Agentic CRM Implementation**
   - Lab 1: 'Job' CRM Template (Automating job applications/recruitment).
   - Lab 2: 'Trade' CRM Template (Automating trading workflows).
   - Lab 3: 'Content-Creator' CRM Template (Automating content production/distribution).
4. **Advanced Agentic Deployment**
   - Scaling autonomous agents.
   - Monitoring and maintenance.
   - Integration with `hanzo.market`.

## Certification
Certification is integrated via `hanzo.market`. Complete all laboratory modules, perform self-verification, and submit your agent's performance metrics for final certification.
