---
title: Pricing
description: What Hanzo Platform costs, and what it does not charge for.
---

**Hanzo Platform is free and self-hostable.** It is the deploy plane: point it at a
repo and it builds, deploys, routes, backs up and monitors the result. Running the
control plane itself costs nothing.

Three things carry a price, and every one of them is served live:

| What | Who bills it | Where the price lives |
|------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Your subscription | Hanzo | `GET https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/billing/plans` |
| Metered AI usage above the included allowance | Hanzo | `GET https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/pricing/models` |
| Hanzo-hosted instances and volumes | Hanzo | `GET https://pricing.hanzo.ai/v1/cloud`, `/v1/gpu` |

[Visor](/docs/services/platform/virtual-machines) can also launch instances **on cloud
accounts you connect with your own API credentials** — AWS, DigitalOcean or Hetzner.
On that path the provider bills you directly at its published rate and Hanzo is not in
the transaction.

## Free credit

New accounts receive **$5 in free credit**, applied automatically, expiring 30 days
after signup. See [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).

and the API disagree, the API is right and the page is stale — open an issue.
