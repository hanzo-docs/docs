Hanzo is an agentic operating system for business, at work since 2014. What
shipping for hundreds of venture-funded startups taught us is what Hanzo's AI
is built on.

## What we have built

- **2014.** Hanzo was founded as Crowdstart, a crowdfunding and commerce
  platform; its first commit landed on 2014-09-29, and it has been Go-native
  since October 2014. Our first customer that year
  was SKULLY, "the fastest fully-funded campaign on Indiegogo, raising \$1
  million in about 45 hours"
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/08/14/the-skully-smart-helmet-is-the-fastest-funded-indiegogo-campaign-to-hit-1m/)).
  AI and machine learning have been in our commercial work since that year.
- **2016.** Crowdstart became Hanzo, adding blockchain and AI-native everything.
- **2017.** We built the first blockchain-native commerce platform. The Unikrn
  ICO raised over 120,000 ETH
  ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/startups/found-ico-makes-sense/);
  [our part](https://hanzo.ai/customers#unikrn)). Hanzo joined Techstars '17
  ([Hanzo Blog, 2017-06-08](https://hanzo.blog/2017-06-08-techstars-17/);
  [Startland News, 2017-10-10](https://startlandnews.com/2017/10/techstars-spotlight-hanzos-e-commerce-tools-designed-to-make-you-money/)).
- **2019.** GPT models in commercial use.
- **2020.** Damon Motorcycles launched at CES on our AI-generated ads: \$8.4M of
  motorcycles sold in 30 days, at a 541:1 return on ad spend
  ([case study](https://hanzo.ai/customers/damon-motorcycles)).
- **The portfolio.** Hundreds of venture-funded startups, with clients funded by
  Y Combinator, 500 Startups, Techstars and other tier-1 venture firms. Billions
  in client revenue. Two companies taken past 100 million users. Two that went
  public on Nasdaq in 2024: Damon Motorcycles
  ([Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/news/95a8e26f/norton-rose-fulbright-advises-grafiti-holding-inc-in-its-business-combination-with-damon-motors-inc))
  and Triller ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/agba-completes-merger-triller-2024-10-15)).

The full timeline, with its case studies and press, is at
[hanzo.ai/about](https://hanzo.ai/about).
