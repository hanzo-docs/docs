**Autopilot replaces brittle cron scripts and unsupervised agent loops with durable, budgeted, approval-gated execution.** Every autopilot flow runs on Hanzo's native automation, workflow, agent, and billing planes under one IAM identity.

## Three autopilot surfaces

| Surface | Canonical endpoints | What it automates | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Business Autopilot](/docs/autopilot/business)** | `POST /v1/auto/automations`, `POST /v1/auto/flows`, `POST /v1/workflow` | Support triage, invoice/billing reactions, webhook event routing, CRM & ledger sync | `shipped` |
| **[Marketing Autopilot](/docs/autopilot/marketing)** | `POST /v1/campaign`, `POST /v1/marketing/audiences`, `POST /v1/marketing/broadcasts`, `POST /v1/content/generate` | Audience segmentation, brand-voice Zen copy, multi-channel launches, A/B campaign optimization | `shipped` |
| **[Agent Workforces](/docs/autopilot/workforces)** | `POST /v1/agent`, `POST /v1/agent/{ref}/run`, `GET /v1/agent/{ref}/spend`, `POST /v1/agent/a2a` | Multi-agent teams with role boundaries, hard spend caps, A2A handoffs, and human escalation | `shipped` |
