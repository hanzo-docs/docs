# AI Accounts

Most people already hold a subscription or two before they arrive. AI Accounts
links those accounts to your Hanzo profile and merges what they report into one
board, so "how much have I used this week" has a single answer instead of three
tabs.

This is a console surface — [console.hanzo.ai](https://console.hanzo.ai) →
**AI Accounts**. For an org bringing its own provider keys so Hanzo can *serve*
those models, see [Connections](/docs/connections) instead.

## Lanes

| Lane | Link it with |
|------|--------------|
| **Hanzo** | Nothing — it is your own account, read from the billing ledger |
| **OpenAI / Codex** | An API key, an OAuth sign-in, or a session cookie header |
| **Anthropic / Claude** | An API key, an OAuth sign-in, or a session cookie header |

The three link modes are `api` (paste a key), `oauth` (sign in), and `web` (paste
a session cookie header — how a consumer subscription with no API key reports its
usage). Whichever you choose, the credential is sealed server-side; the browser
never holds it, and the accounts list only ever returns a masked label.

## Overview

The overview merges both sides of the feed. Provider lanes report what their own
usage APIs expose — session and weekly quota consumed, cost, tokens, and
requests. The Hanzo lane comes from the real commerce ledger over the last 30
days, with your top models broken out.

A lane that cannot be read says so rather than showing a zero: the board fails
soft per lane, so one unreachable provider never blanks the rest.

## Machines

Usage arrives from wherever you work — desktop, CLI, web. The Machines tab shows
the surfaces reporting under your account, which is how the same subscription
counted twice on two laptops becomes visible.

## Routing

The routing toggle is your **preference** for whether Hanzo's smart routing is
used on your sessions. It resolves against the org: if your organization has
auto routing switched off, that wins and the toggle is disabled; otherwise your
choice overrides the org default.

It is a preference, not a policy edit. Calls that explicitly ask for
`"model": "auto"` still follow the platform routing flag — the policy itself
lives in [Router](/docs/router).

## Accounts

Link, relink, and remove lanes here. Removing a lane deletes the stored
credential and drops that lane from the board; historical Hanzo usage is
unaffected, because it was never the provider's to begin with.
