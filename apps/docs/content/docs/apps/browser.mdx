# Browser extensions

AI chat, page understanding, and one-click actions on any tab — summarize a page, extract data, fill a form, or ask about what you're looking at, all without leaving the browser.

## Availability

The extension is in no browser store yet. Its builds for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge are on [hanzo.ai/download](https://hanzo.ai/download) (and [GitHub](https://github.com/hanzoai/extension/releases/latest)); load it from your browser's extensions page.

With nothing to install, [Hanzo Chat](https://hanzo.app) works in any browser: sign in with Hanzo, paste or drop in the page you are reading, and ask.

> **One identity, everywhere.** Sign in with Hanzo ([IAM](/docs/openapi/iam) OAuth) or paste an `sk-` [API key](/docs/api-keys). The same credential works in every Hanzo surface — see [Integrations & Extensions](/docs/apps).
