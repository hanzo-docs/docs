# Design tools

Bring Hanzo into your canvas — generate and rewrite copy, name and annotate layers, and run AI actions on a selection in Figma and Sketch.

## Availability

The Figma and Sketch plugins are not published: their builds are not public, so there is nothing to install.

> **One identity, everywhere.** Sign in with Hanzo ([IAM](/docs/openapi/iam) OAuth) or paste an `sk-` [API key](/docs/api-keys). The same credential works in every Hanzo surface — see [Integrations & Extensions](/docs/apps).
