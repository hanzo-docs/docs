# Docs & Office

Draft, summarize, rewrite, and analyze documents where they already live — Microsoft Office, Outlook, Google Workspace, and PDFs.

## Microsoft Office and Outlook

A task-pane add-in for Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and a reading-pane add-in for Outlook. Neither is published: their manifests are not public, so there is nothing to sideload.

## Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Gmail)

Draft, summarize, and edit across Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Gmail — no download; connect over OAuth.

**Connect** — open the [console](https://console.hanzo.ai), pick **Google Workspace**, and authorize with your Google account. Hanzo then appears in the Workspace side panel across Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Gmail.

## PDF

Summarize, query, and extract structured data from PDFs in [Hanzo Chat](https://chat.hanzo.ai) — sign in with Hanzo and drop in a file.

> **One identity, everywhere.** Sign in with Hanzo ([IAM](/docs/openapi/iam) OAuth) or paste an `sk-` [API key](/docs/api-keys). The same credential works in every Hanzo surface — see [Integrations & Extensions](/docs/apps).
