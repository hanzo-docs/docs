You earn points for real work: trying models, building, referring builders who stay,
contributing to Hanzo's open source, and posts that stay up. Points set your level and your
place on [hanzo.ai/leaderboard](https://hanzo.ai/leaderboard). Points from reviewed work can
be redeemed for Hanzo AI service credit.

## What a point is

- A point is a reputation mark in Hanzo's ledger. It belongs to one account.
- It cannot be transferred, sold or sent.
- It is not a token and not money, and it has no cash value.
- It is not an investment. Nothing here promises a return.
- Hanzo pays no cash and sends no tokens for points. The only thing points buy is service
  credit in your own account, and only some points do: [Redeem points](/docs/contributing/redeem).

## Levels

Five levels, in order: **Hacker, Builder, Shipper, Core, Legend.** Your lifetime points set
your level. Core and Legend also need reviewed work that is not a referral: a merged
contribution, an approved bounty or integration, or a security report. No amount of referring
reaches them alone.

## Two balances

**Lifetime points** count everything you earned. They set your level, your rank and your
badge progress, and they never go down.

**Redeemable points** are the part earned from reviewed work, less what you already redeemed.
Redeeming never lowers your level or rank.

## Ways to earn

Each one pays once per accomplishment unless it says otherwise.

| Source | You earn it by |
|---|---|
| Exploring models and modalities | Your first successful task of each kind (text, vision, image, audio, reasoning), and trying more models. A model counts after a successful task that does real work. Aliases of one model do not count twice. |
| Building and deploying | A tool call, an MCP tool, an agent task, a CLI task, a sandbox run, a deployed application, and steady days of building. A published integration or a reusable template earns after review. |
| Referrals | Someone you referred does a first real task, then comes back and uses Hanzo again. They get a welcome award on their first qualified task. A person you referred still counts toward your badges after you reach the referral cap. |
| Community | A testimonial or a showcase that Hanzo reviews and accepts. |
| Open source and bounties | Merged pull requests, bug fixes, docs, translations, accepted issues, issues you reported that closed as completed, bounties, and valid security reports. [Bounties](/docs/contributing/bounties). |
| Verified posts | A public post with your own link that is still up after the waiting window. [Share a post](/docs/contributing/share). |
| Token milestones | One-time awards as your recorded usage passes each milestone. Reputation only, never redeemable, one badge each. There are no recurring points per token. |
| Beta | Applying to the beta, and being accepted into Alpha. |

Some kinds are part of the program and award nothing yet, because their event is not measured
and verified. The rules mark them `live: false`.

## What never earns

- Visits to your link, sign-ups, and stars or follows on their own.
- Staff and bots of every participating organization.
- Self referrals, circular referrals, and accounts that belong to one person.
- Usage Hanzo does not meter.
- Sourcing investors or financing.

The rules table lists the same set (`excluded`).

## Earlier points

Points from the earlier beta board are grandfathered: each member's total is kept whole as one
legacy award. It counts toward your lifetime points and your level. It is not redeemable.

## Read the rules

One table holds every value: what each kind earns, the caps, the level thresholds, the token
milestones, the redemption limits and the post window. Every page and tool reads it. These docs
copy none of it, so they cannot go stale. No key is needed.

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules
```

**Levels.** Name, lifetime points, and whether it also needs reviewed non-referral work:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq -r '.levels[] | [.name, .points, .substantive] | @tsv'
```

**What a kind earns.** One kind by id, or every kind in a source (`explore`, `build`,
`referrals`, `community`, `share`, `contributions`, `usage`, `beta`):

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq '.kinds[] | select(.id == "merged") | {title, points, rule}'

curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq -r '.kinds[] | select(.source == "explore") | [.points, .title] | @tsv'
```

**The caps.** The most a person earns from a source in a window:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.caps'
```

**Token milestones.** Tokens, points, and the badge each unlocks:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.tiers'
```

**Which points redeem**, and which kinds are not live yet:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq -r '.kinds[] | select(.credit) | .title'
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq -r '.kinds[] | select(.live | not) | .title'
```

**The redemption rate and limits**, and **the post window**:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.redeem'
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.share'
```

A kind with `manual: true` is awarded by a person after review, and a kind with
`private: true` stays off the public board. Every award records the table `version` it was
priced by, so a change never rewrites what you already earned.

## Your standing

Signed in, `GET https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/me` answers your rank, points, level, the next
level, badges, redeemable points, and every award behind them.

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/me -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY"
```

[hanzo.ai/leaderboard](https://hanzo.ai/leaderboard) is the public board. Your name shows on it
only if you choose it (`PUT /v1/rewards/optin`); otherwise you rank as a builder. Every
endpoint is listed in the [Rewards API](/docs/contributing/api).
