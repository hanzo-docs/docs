Points from reviewed work redeem for Hanzo AI service credit. The credit lands in your own
account, in whole dollars, and you spend it on Hanzo. It is not cash, it cannot be sent to anyone,
and it has no cash value.

## Which points redeem

Only points from verified, reviewed work, earned under this program:

- retained referrals
- reviewed open source contributions
- approved bounties
- approved published integrations
- security reports, after manual approval

These do not redeem: exploration, token milestones, beta and Alpha, verified posts, and points
carried over from the earlier beta board. They still count toward your level.

The rules mark every kind that redeems with `credit: true`:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq -r '.kinds[] | select(.credit) | [.id, .title] | @tsv'
```

Your **redeemable points** are those points less what you already redeemed. `GET /v1/rewards/me`
returns them as `redeemable`. Redeeming spends redeemable points only. Your lifetime points,
level and rank do not change.

## The rate and the limits

How many points make one dollar, how much one member can redeem in a calendar month, and how much
the whole program can redeem in a month are in the rules. Money fields there are in cents.

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.redeem'
```

Months are calendar months in UTC. `GET /v1/rewards/me` also returns your own `allowance`: what
your month allows, what you used, and what is left.

## Redeem

Ask for whole dollars:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/redeem \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"dollars": 1}'
```

`dollars` is a whole number from one up to your monthly limit. The request reserves the points and
the budget in one step, so two requests at once cannot spend the same points or pass a limit.
Then the credit is granted to your account. The answer:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | The redemption. |
| `points`, `cents` | What was spent, and what it bought. |
| `status` | `granted`, or `reserved` while the credit is finishing. |
| `at` | When you asked. |
| `redeemable` | Your redeemable points after this one. |
| `allowance` | Your month: `monthCents`, `usedCents`, `leftCents`, and `open`. |

If `status` is `reserved`, the credit is on its way and is granted once. Do not ask again. If the
credit cannot be granted, your points are returned and the request answers 502.

Your redemptions, newest first:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/redemptions -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY"
```

## When a request is refused

| Status | Why |
|---|---|
| 400 | `dollars` is not a whole number in range, or your account has no username to credit. |
| 401 | You are not signed in. |
| 403 | Staff do not redeem. |
| 409 | You asked for more than your redeemable points. Or more than your month allows. Or redeeming is not open. Or the program's budget for the month is spent. |
| 502 | The credit could not be granted. Your points were returned. |

## When the month's budget is spent

The program has a fixed redemption budget each calendar month, so total redemptions cannot run past it. When
it is spent, a redemption answers 409 and says so. Your points are untouched, and `allowance.open`
is false. The budget opens again next month.

## What it is not

No cash. No payout. No transfer to another account or person. Credit has no cash value, and
nothing redeems into a token. See [Credits & billing](/docs/credits) for how credit is spent.
