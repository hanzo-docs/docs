A badge is a collectible that records something you did: a first task, ten models, a merged
pull request, a usage milestone. You unlock it automatically when your awards meet its rule. You
need no wallet and you claim nothing to have it.

## What a badge is

- A badge **grants no points of its own.** Points come only from the event that unlocks it, if
  that event earns any ([Hanzo Rewards](/docs/contributing/earn)). Claiming a badge on a chain
  grants none either.
- A badge is a record of achievement. It is not an investment and not a token reward.
- Each badge is locked, unlocked, or claimed.
- Higher usage milestones get more elaborate art. They do not get more money.

## Shelves and rarity

Badges sit on five shelves:

| Shelf | What it records |
|---|---|
| `explorer` | The models and modalities you tried. |
| `technology` | The Hanzo tools you used: Zen, Kai, Enso, Kata, the CLI, MCP, sandboxes, deploys, integrations and templates. |
| `usage` | The token milestones your usage passed. |
| `community` | Beta and Alpha, referrals that stuck, merged work, bounties, security. |
| `mastery` | Steady building, open source standing, and levels. |

Each badge has a rarity: `common`, `uncommon`, `rare`, `epic` or `legendary`.

The rules table lists every badge with its shelf, rarity and the rule that unlocks it:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq -r '.badges[] | [.group, .rarity, .id, .rule] | @tsv'
```

One shelf, or one rarity:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq -r '.badges[] | select(.group == "usage") | [.rarity, .name] | @tsv'

curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules \
  | jq -r '.badges[] | select(.rarity == "legendary") | .name'
```

A badge with `live: false` is on the shelf but cannot unlock yet: the event that unlocks it is
not measured and verified. A badge turns live the day its event does.

## Your badges

`GET /v1/rewards/me` lists every badge, with `earned` and, once earned, `at`.

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/me -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY" \
  | jq '.badges[] | select(.earned)'
```

Missions are guides toward a badge, such as trying models across modalities or deploying an
application. A mission awards nothing of its own, and finishing one duplicates no points already granted for
its steps:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.missions'
```

## Claim on the Hanzo network

Claiming a badge on the Hanzo network (hanzo.network, Hanzo's own EVM chain, gas in the native
AI coin) is coming and is off until the badge contract is live.

When it opens you will connect a verified wallet and claim a badge you already unlocked. Each
claim is one non-transferable token for one person and one badge: a locked ERC-721 under
ERC-5192, so it cannot be sold or sent. Hanzo pays the gas in the native AI coin from a
sponsored budget. The coin is separate from points. Points are never a coin.

The rules say whether claiming is open:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.chain'
```

While `minting` is `false`, nothing mints. Each badge's metadata and art are served either way.

## Metadata and art

Each badge has a public address for its metadata and one for its art. No key is needed.

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/badges/first-spark
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/badges/first-spark/art
```

`GET /v1/rewards/badges/{id}` answers the badge's metadata. `GET /v1/rewards/badges/{id}/art`
answers its image. Badge ids are the `id` values in the rules.

The metadata describes the badge and nothing else. It holds no personal data: no name, no
account, no address and no usage count. Two people with the same badge have the same metadata.
