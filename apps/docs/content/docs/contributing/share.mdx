A post earns [Hanzo Rewards](/docs/contributing/earn) points only when it is proven and has stayed
up. You paste its URL. Hanzo reads the post itself, holds it as Pending, reads it again after the
waiting window, and awards the points once if the post is still up and still has your link.

## Share and earn

1. Copy your own link from [hanzo.ai/referral](https://hanzo.ai/referral).
2. Post it, on your own public account, with the disclosure below.
3. Paste the post's URL into **Share and earn** on [hanzo.ai/referral](https://hanzo.ai/referral)
   or [hanzo.ai/leaderboard](https://hanzo.ai/leaderboard).

Your posts list shows each one as Pending (with the days left), Confirmed or Removed. A post
that waits on a maintainer shows as Pending review. The points still waiting show next to your
total as a pending amount.

## What Hanzo checks

- **Hanzo reads the post, not your browser.** The address is fetched on the server, from the
  network's own public endpoint.
- **The post is public** and carries your own link. Redirects and short links are followed, and
  the match is on your code.
- **It is your post.** Your own code in the link is the proof. A post whose author Hanzo cannot
  tell, or whose author another member already holds, goes to review instead of earning.
- **After the waiting window** Hanzo reads it again. Still up, with your link: it earns once.
  Deleted, edited, or made private: it is marked Removed and earns nothing.
- **Private or locked accounts earn nothing,** because Hanzo cannot read them.
- **One post, one award.** The same post is claimed once. The same words on a second network do
  not stack.
- **There is a limit** on posts that count per person in a rolling window, and a cap on share
  points. Self-referral blocks apply, and staff and bots earn nothing.

A pending post is a claim, not points. It cannot rank and cannot be redeemed. Points from posts
are reputation and do not redeem.

The window, the limit and what a post is worth are in the rules:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.share'
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.kinds[] | select(.id == "share_verified")'
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq '.caps[] | select(.source == "share")'
```

## Networks

Hanzo reads these on its own, with no sign-in on your side:

- X
- Bluesky
- Reddit
- Hacker News
- Mastodon

These wait for review. A maintainer confirms them by hand after the window, because Hanzo cannot
read them without your sign-in on that network:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook

The list is `share.networks` in the rules, with a `verified` flag on each.

## Say you are rewarded

If you post about Hanzo and earn points for it, say so in the post:

> I earn Hanzo Rewards points.

This is an endorsement for a reward, and it must be disclosed (FTC Endorsement Guides). The
exact line is in the rules:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/rules | jq -r '.disclosure'
```

## API

Paste a post:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/posts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"url": "https://bsky.app/profile/you.example/post/3kabc"}'
```

List your posts and what the waiting ones are worth:

```bash
curl -s https://api.hanzo.ai/v1/rewards/posts -H "Authorization: Bearer $HANZO_API_KEY"
```

The answer is `posts`, newest first, and `pending`, the points still waiting. It is not in your
points. Each post carries:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | The claim. |
| `network` | `x`, `bluesky`, `reddit`, `hackernews`, `mastodon`, `linkedin` or `facebook`. |
| `url` | The post's address. |
| `status` | `pending`, `confirmed`, `removed`, or `review` (waiting on a maintainer). |
| `points` | What it earned, or will earn if it counts. 0 when removed. |
| `at` | When you pasted it. |
| `countsAt`, `daysLeft` | When it counts, and the whole days until then. |
| `note` | Why a post earned less or nothing, or what it waits on. |

A URL on a host Hanzo does not read is refused. Hanzo fetches only each network's own address.
