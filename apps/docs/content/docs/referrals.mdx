The **Hanzo Referral & Affiliate Program** empowers developers, creators, content publishers, agencies, and cloud architects to monetize their technical recommendations.

<Callout>
**Dual-benefit structure**: When someone signs up using your affiliate link, they receive **$20 in immediate platform credits**, and you earn **15% recurring commission** on all net infrastructure spend they generate for their first 12 months.
</Callout>

## Program Structure & Economics

| Incentive | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Referee Welcome Credits** | **$20 USD** (2,000 cents) | Granted automatically to referred organizations upon signup to explore models, GPUs, and cloud services. |
| **Referrer Commission** | **15%** | Recurring revenue share on all net platform compute, storage, GPU, and API spend. |
| **Commission Window** | **12 Months** | 365 days of recurring commissions from the date of the referred account's initial signup. |
| **Attribution Model** | **First-Touch** | Bound permanently to your partner code when the referred user first accesses the platform. |

## How Attribution Works

1. **Shareable Links**: Mint custom vanity links in the Hanzo Console (e.g., `https://hanzo.ai/?aff=YOUR_CODE`).
2. **First-Touch Capture**: When a visitor clicks your link, their browser captures the affiliate token in secure local storage across authentication redirects.
3. **Account Binding**: Upon signup or organization creation, `/v1/affiliates/attribute` permanently links the new organization to your partner account.
4. **Transparent Ledger**: Review real-time click volume, conversion rates, and accrued commission by period under **Settings → Affiliates** in Hanzo Console.

## Payout Methods & Thresholds

Earnings accrue continuously and settle on the 1st of each month for balances exceeding $50:

- **HUSD Stablecoin (Hanzo EVM)**: Gasless instant settlement on the sovereign blockchain.
- **Direct Crypto Rails**: Automatic transfers in ETH, USDC, or sovereign network tokens.
- **Stripe Connect & ACH/Wire**: Direct fiat bank deposits in 130+ supported currencies.
- **Hanzo Cloud Credits**: 1:1 conversion into compute credits with an additional 10% bonus credit multiplier.

## Program Rules & Guidelines

- **No Self-Referrals**: Commissions are not paid on accounts owned, operated, or affiliated with the referrer.
- **Fair Promotion**: Paid search ads targeting branded keywords (e.g. "Hanzo", "Hanzo Cloud", "Hanzo AI") are prohibited without prior authorization.
- **Developer Integrity**: Spamming forums, repositories, or social communities is strictly prohibited and results in immediate account suspension.

## How to Join

1. Sign in to your account at [console.hanzo.ai](https://console.hanzo.ai).
2. Open **Settings → Affiliates**.
3. Choose your custom vanity code (e.g. `yourname`) and click **Generate Link**.
4. Share your link in project documentation, blogs, videos, and tutorials.
