The following 10 enterprise blueprints represent verified production architectures combining **Kai** (calibrated multimodal decision models), **Zen** (open-weight foundation models), **Enso** (microsecond learned utility routing), and **Kata / Gen / Dev** (autonomous software engineering loops).

All architectures operate under sovereign enterprise controls: edge inference at the shelf and cross-dock, local or on-premise DGX clusters, and governed cloud escalation.

---

## 10 Enterprise Production Blueprints

### 01. Shelf & Ghost Inventory Triage
*Multimodal edge vision detects misplaced SKUs, out-of-stock items, and occluded facings under store lighting variations.*

- **Stack**: `Kai-M + Kai`
- **Economic Value**: **5–20 bps sales recovery** (Retail availability engine)
- **Architecture**: Edge-mounted camera feeds process shelf frames into discrete bounding boxes via Kai-M. Kai evaluates inventory state distributions into discrete restock priority classes with zero output tokens and sub-90ms latency.
- **📄 White Paper / Specification**: [HIP-1332: Kai Decision Model Specification](https://hips.hanzo.ai/docs/hip-1332-kai-the-decision-model)
- **📰 Research Blog Post**: [Kai: One Intelligence. Every Modality. Every Decision.](https://hanzo.blog/blog/2026-10-16-kai-unified-multimodal/)
- **💻 Open Source Recipe**: [`recipes/src/retail.rs` (`ShelfInventoryRecipe`)](https://github.com/hanzoai/kai/tree/main/recipes)

```rust
use recipes::retail::ShelfInventoryRecipe;

let triage = ShelfInventoryRecipe::new("AISLE-14-BEV", 0.08, 3);
let decision = triage.evaluate(&client).await?;
if decision.is_restock_required() {
    dispatch_associate_alert("Aisle 14 bay 3: replenish facing");
}
```

### 02. Cross-Dock Pallet Anomaly Triage
*Real-time dock sensors identify pallet tilt, crush damage, and stretch-wrap tears before ingestion into automated storage and retrieval systems (ASRS).*

- **Stack**: `Kai-M + Kata`
- **Economic Value**: **$100M–$500M annual logistics value** ($0.001–$0.005 net value per item across billions of items)
- **Architecture**: Forklift and gantry camera vision streams detect pallet structural defects. Kai outputs calibrated disposition probabilities (Accept, Inspect, Repalletize, Quarantine) under site safety rules.
- **📄 White Paper / Specification**: [HIP-1334: Unified Research & Edge Sensor Runtime](https://hips.hanzo.ai/docs/hip-1334-research-unified-runtime)
- **📰 Research Blog Post**: [Kai 1: Decisions, Not Completions](https://hanzo.blog/blog/2026-10-07-introducing-kai/)
- **💻 Open Source Recipe**: [`recipes/src/industrial.rs` (`PalletTriageRecipe`)](https://github.com/hanzoai/kai/tree/main/recipes)

### 03. High-Value Returns Fraud Inspection
*Identifies item swaps, missing serial tags, internal component tampering, and automated restock vs. liquidator routing.*

- **Architecture**: High-resolution image diffs compare returned merchandise against pristine golden SKU catalogs. Visual embeddings generate anomaly vectors; Kai scores disposition confidence with zero hallucinations.

### 09. Autonomous CI/CD Test Repair
*Continuous test suite maintenance on autopilot: captures test failures, identifies root causes in the AST, applies surgical diffs, and verifies green passes.*

- **Stack**: `Kata + Gen + Enso`
- **Economic Value**: **$55M–$552M developer capacity value** (2–5 hrs/week saved per engineer).
- **Architecture**: Hooks into GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. On test failure, Kata extracts traces, Kai triages the regression, Enso routes AST diff generation to optimal models, and candidate patches are verified in isolated microVMs before opening pull requests.
- **📄 White Paper / Specification**: [HIP-0510: Closed-Loop Agentic Verification](https://hips.hanzo.ai/docs/hip-0510-enso-learned-router)
- **📰 Research Blog Post**: [Closed-Loop Autonomous Test Repair: From Stack Trace to Green PR](https://hanzo.blog/blog/2026-10-16-closed-loop-agentic-repair/)
- **💻 Open Source Recipe**: [`recipes/src/repair.rs` (`AutomatedTestRepairLoop`)](https://github.com/hanzoai/kata/tree/main/recipes)

### 10. AI Inference Cost & Latency Optimization
*Learned microsecond routing balances quality, latency budgets, and token cost across local models, open weights, and frontier cloud providers.*

- **Stack**: `Enso + Kai + Zen`
- **Economic Value**: **Up to 90% cloud compute cost reduction** across enterprise LLM traffic.
- **Architecture**: Microsecond learned utility router (`utility = quality - λ·cost - μ·latency`) evaluates prompt features on CPU in 12 µs. Routes high-volume structured queries to open weights and reserves frontier models for complex multi-step reasoning.
- **📄 White Paper / Specification**: [Hanzo Router: Learned Bilinear Utility Routing](https://papers.hanzo.ai/hanzo-router/)
- **📰 Research Blog Post**: [Route every prompt — and cut AI costs by up to 90%](https://hanzo.blog/blog/2026-07-07-hanzo-router-cut-ai-costs/)
- **💻 Open Source Recipe**: [`recipes/src/routing.rs` (`CostLatencyOptimizer`)](https://github.com/hanzoai/enso/tree/main/recipes)
