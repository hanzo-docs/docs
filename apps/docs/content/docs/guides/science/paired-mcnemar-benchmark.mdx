**Unpaired leaderboard percentages lie whenever two models complete different item subsets.** `GET /v1/benchmark/compare` is the only valid head-to-head test: it pairs both models on `n_common` and computes an exact McNemar test.

## Architecture & contract

For every item both Arm A and Arm B completed, the arena tallies `rescue` (B passed, A failed) and `damage` (A passed, B failed) and outputs the two-sided exact McNemar `p`-value. Never declare a winner when `n_common` is too small or `p >= 0.05`.

**Primary endpoint:** `GET /v1/benchmark/compare` · **Status:** `shipped` · **Level:** `practitioner`

## Verify the outcome

- Check `n_common > 0`, `rescue`, `damage`, and `p_value` before making any model swap decision.

## Production guardrails

- Never blend vendor claims with standardized harness scores; `GET /v1/benchmark/leaderboard` prints both columns side by side for this reason.
