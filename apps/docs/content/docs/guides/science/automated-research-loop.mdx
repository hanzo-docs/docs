**Replace manual overnight scripts with event-driven Research Autopilot.** Whenever a study finishes scoring a baseline vs. candidate run, Hanzo wakes a sandboxed agent to investigate regressions or draft the next bounded experiment.

## Architecture & contract

The automation engine deduplicates `research / improvement.reviewed` events by exact evidence hash, injects study payloads as untrusted data, and enforces explicit `permissions: "ask"` so no autonomous research loop merges unreviewed code.

**Primary endpoint:** `POST /v1/auto/automations` · **Status:** `preview` · **Level:** `specialist`

## Verify the outcome

- Confirm the automation is armed (`GET /v1/auto/automations`) and trigger one dry run via `POST /v1/auto/automations/{id}/run`.

## Production guardrails

- Keep `permissions: "ask"` on every research automation; never grant silent production deploy tokens.
