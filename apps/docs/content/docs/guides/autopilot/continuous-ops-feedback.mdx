**Your human review queue is your highest-leverage training dataset.** Every time an operator overrides a routed ticket or edits a generated response, emit one structured correction so next week's autopilot makes fewer mistakes.

## Architecture & contract

Decision overrides flow into `/v1/decisions/{decision}/corrections` (feeding `POST /v1/capabilities/{name}/train`), while generative edits flow into `POST /v1/ai/feedback` (feeding Enso's learned router). Both stay strictly inside your org.

**Primary endpoint:** `POST /v1/decisions/{decision}/corrections` · **Status:** `shipped` · **Level:** `specialist`

## Verify the outcome

- Verify `GET /v1/capabilities/corrections` increments captured counts for your target question key.

## Production guardrails

- Ensure capture is enabled (`PUT /v1/capabilities/capture`) before collecting overrides so rows are marked `captured` rather than `missing`.
