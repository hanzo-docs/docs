---
title: Why calibrated decisions
description: Understand why Kai answers with calibrated probabilities over options you define instead of generated text, and what calibration does and does not promise.
---

**A generative model answers a bounded question by writing text. Kai answers it
with a probability for each answer you allow, which code can branch on, sort and
gate without parsing anything.**

## Text is the wrong output for a decision

| Generated text | A typed decision |
|---|---|
| The answer sits somewhere in prose, and may not match any label you expected. | The answer is one of your options, with a probability for each. |
| Asked how sure it is, the model writes more text, and nothing ties that number to how often it is right. | The probabilities are fitted to how often answers turn out right. |
| Every word written is paid for. | Nothing is written: `usage.output_tokens` is 0. |
| The same prompt can come back worded differently. | The same request comes back with the same answer ([Typed decisions](/docs/kai/concepts)). |

## What Kai computes

Kai reads the question and the state with a multilingual encoder, reads each
option on its own, and scores every option against them. A choice's
probabilities are a softmax over its options' scores. A noul compares the score
of its true side with that of its false side. A score's levels are options, and
`score` is the expected level. Nothing is generated and nothing is sampled.
[Kai](/docs/models/kai) describes the model.

## Calibration

A model is **calibrated** when, of the answers it gives a probability p, about a
share p are right. It describes many answers, not one: an answer at 0.8 is wrong
about one time in five, and that is the model working as intended.

- **Training.** Kai learns from labelled decisions under the log loss and the
  Brier score. Both are proper scoring rules: their expected value is lowest
  when the stated probability equals the true rate. On ordered levels, the
  ranked probability score takes the Brier score's place.
- **Fitting.** After training, one temperature per question type and option
  count is fitted on held-out validation data. `routing.calibration` names the
  fitted tables behind each answer.
- **Measuring.** Expected calibration error groups answers by their stated
  probability, compares each group's average probability with its accuracy, and
  averages the gaps by group size. Kai's per-suite accuracy and calibration error
  are published with its
  [harness results](https://github.com/hanzoai/benchmarks/tree/c61361fedc5882b0d83b2f5d5a6a35c56ffeb3c3/decision/results/kai-a7):
  on that harness, Kai (checkpoint a7) is more accurate than Jev on 11 of 12
  suites, and Jev leads jailbreak detection, 0.940 to 0.920.

Calibration holds on data like the data it was fitted on. Questions worded
differently or about other cases can move it, so check it on your own outcomes
before a threshold goes live: [Confidence and thresholds](/docs/kai/confidence).

## When text is the right output

When the answer cannot be listed in advance (a reply, a summary, code, an
explanation), the output is text, and a [Zen](/docs/models/zen) model writes it
through the same API. The two compose: Kai decides whether a case needs writing
and where it goes, and Zen writes it
([Triage with Kai, reply with Zen](/docs/guides/cookbook/triage)).
