**Claims without paired measurements and pinned provenance are noise.** Hanzo Science turns prompt edits, model upgrades, agent harness patches, and routing changes into falsifiable experiments with cryptographic reproducibility.

## Four empirical planes

| Plane | Canonical endpoints | What it guarantees | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Experiments** | `POST /v1/experiment`, `GET /v1/experiment/{id}/assign`, `POST /v1/experiment/{id}/analyze`, `POST /v1/experiment/{id}/decide` | Deterministic subject bucketing, controlled outcome analysis, audited winner promotion | `shipped` |
| **Benchmarks** | `GET /v1/benchmark/catalog`, `GET /v1/benchmark/leaderboard`, `GET /v1/benchmark/compare`, `GET /v1/benchmark/history` | Standardized harness measurements separated from vendor claims; exact-McNemar paired comparisons (`n_common`) | `shipped` |
| **Evaluations** | `POST /v1/eval/datasets`, `POST /v1/eval/evaluators`, `GET /v1/eval/runs`, `GET /v1/eval/metrics` | Golden datasets, reusable Kai/model judges, trace-linked scores, latency & cost percentiles | `shipped` |
| **Research studies** | `GET /v1/research/runs` (public live), `POST /v1/research/studies`, `GET /v1/research/actions` | Versioned improvement studies, confidence-interval gating, draft findings, `improvement.reviewed` events | `preview` |
