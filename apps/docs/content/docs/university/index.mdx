**[Hanzo University](https://hanzo.university)** is the engineering curriculum and hands-on lab system for building, training, evaluating, securing, and monetizing production AI systems on Hanzo. Every course pairs technical readings on `docs.hanzo.ai` with runnable interactive workspaces on **[hanzo.app/learn](https://hanzo.app/learn)**.

## Four Schools, 25 Courses

## Six Certifications

Each certification validates practical lab execution and graded verification harnesses on live Hanzo infrastructure:

| # | Certification | Required courses | Primary verification artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hanzo Certified AI Practitioner** | `ENG 100`, `AUTO 101`, `PRA 104` | End-to-end 4-surface CLI/SDK/HTTP/MCP workflow + verified MCP skill |
| 2 | **Hanzo Certified Decision & Intelligence Engineer** | `KAI 201`, `KAI 301`, `ENS 201`, `ZEN 201` | Trained & published Kai business capability passing 5-axis eval + Enso/Zen loop |
| 3 | **Hanzo Certified ML Systems Architect** | `ENS 301`, `ZEN 301`, `RL 101`, `ML 401`, `ARC 105` | Fine-tuned Zen adapter + GRPO reward loop + reproducible SHA-256 registry promotion |
| 4 | **Hanzo Certified Research & Discovery Engineer** | `SCI 101`, `SCI 201`, `SCI 301`, `SCI 401` | Controlled experiment + paired McNemar benchmark report + outcome-triggered study automation |
| 5 | **Hanzo Certified Sovereign Security Engineer** | `CYB 101`, `CYB 201`, `CYB 301`, `CYB 401`, `SYS 103` | Enforced Kai command gate + sandboxed exploit reproducer + zero-trust KMS/AuthZ deployment |
| 6 | **Hanzo Certified Autonomous Enterprise Architect** | `MKT 102`, `AUTO 201`, `AUTO 301`, `ENT 401` | Multi-tenant white-label AI SaaS with metered billing, agent workforce, and marketing autopilot |
