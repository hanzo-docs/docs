**Never fine-tune a 27B generative model when a 50-row CPU decision capability or a routing reward fixes the problem.** Hanzo organizes training into three distinct planes ordered by speed, cost, and blast radius.

## Three training planes

| Plane | What changes | Hardware & speed | Canonical endpoint | Best for | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[Kai Capabilities](/docs/training/kai-capabilities)** | Org-private calibration, linear head, or boost+veto pair over frozen Kai representations | CPU, seconds, zero re-encoding of captured states | `POST /v1/capabilities/{name}/train` | Domain-specific classification, support triage, fraud/risk gating, policy verdicts | `shipped` |
| **[Enso Routing](/docs/training/enso-routing)** | Per-org learned bilinear routing utility and custom escalation blends | CPU, online reward ledger + held-out candidate promotion | `POST /v1/ai/feedback`, `POST /v1/benchmark/presets` | Cutting inference spend, hitting latency SLOs, preferring models that succeed on your tasks | `shipped` |
| **[Zen Fine-Tuning & RL](/docs/training/zen-fine-tuning)** | LoRA, QLoRA, full weights, or on-policy RL adapters on open-weight Zen models | Managed GPU or BYO fleet, metered per device-second | `POST /v1/train/jobs`, `POST /v1/train/clients` | Proprietary writing style, domain code synthesis, specialized reasoning & tool schemas | `shipped` |

## Shared safety & provenance invariants

Every training plane on Hanzo obeys four non-negotiable guarantees:

1. **Tenant isolation by default**: Captured states, corrections, feedback rewards, datasets, and trained weights belong strictly to your org. Base models never absorb another customer's data.
2. **Held-out evaluation before publish**: A candidate cannot be published unless it clears its evaluation gate against the incumbent or base model (with explicit override audit when forced on GPU jobs).
3. **Bit-for-bit blast-radius containment**: A Kai capability routes only behind its exact question key, type, and labels — every other question in the same request stays bit-for-bit identical to base Kai.
4. **Instant rollback without retraining**: Every published artifact is content-addressed by SHA-256. Rolling back (`POST /v1/capabilities/{name}/rollback`) swaps the active pointer immediately.
