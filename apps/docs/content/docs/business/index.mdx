**Don't spend six months rebuilding IAM, multi-tenant billing ledgers, model routing, and compliance evidence before signing your first paying customer.** Hanzo gives AI founders the entire commercial operating system behind one `/v1/` API.

## Commercial architecture blocks

| Capability | Canonical endpoints | Business role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[White-Label Multi-Tenancy](/docs/business/white-label)** | `/v1/iam/organizations`, `/v1/base/bases`, `/v1/plan`, `/v1/entitlement` | Isolated customer workspaces, SSO, custom domains, per-plan feature gates | `shipped` |
| **[Private Intelligence](/docs/business/private-intelligence)** | `/v1/capabilities/{name}/train`, `/v1/train/jobs`, `/v1/benchmark/presets` | Turn proprietary domain workflows into defensible, tenant-isolated Kai & Zen models | `shipped` |
| **[Monetize APIs & Tools](/docs/business/monetize-api)** | `/v1/billing/*`, `/v1/commerce/*`, `/v1/marketplace/listings`, `/v1/licensing/*` | Usage metering, prepaid credits, invoices, marketplace distribution & license keys | `shipped` |
| **[AI SaaS Reference Blueprint](/docs/business/ai-saas)** | `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/decisions`, `/v1/event`, `/v1/deploy/applications` | End-to-end architecture from first commit to metered revenue and enterprise SOC 2 | `shipped` |
